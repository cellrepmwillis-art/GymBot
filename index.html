          For now the trainer follows built-in rules based on published guidance (sources are listed under each workout), not a live AI. It isn't medical advice.

          CookBot on this site already has a key.

          Ask the trainer sends your question and today's workout to the Anthropic API (Claude Sonnet 5.5) using your own key from console.anthropic.com. A question costs about a penny. Everything else in GymBot is free and works offline.

          The week is push, pull, legs, twice, Monday to Saturday, with Sunday off. The time of day decides how long the session is and how busy the gym is likely to be. A workout after midnight counts toward the day before, until 3am. Your settings and the days you locked in are stored only in this browser.
