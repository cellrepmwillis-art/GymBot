            Clearing Chrome's "Cookies and site data" erases GymBot's settings, locked workouts and calories. Back up first, then restore after.

            The file includes your API key, if you added one, so keep it to yourself.

            Ask the trainer sends your question and today's workout to the Anthropic API (Claude Sonnet 5.5) using your own key from console.anthropic.com. A question costs about a penny. Everything else in GymBot is free and works offline.

            Calories burned are estimates from the Compendium of Physical Activities: the effort of each part of the workout, its minutes and your weight. Real burn varies from person to person.

            The week is push, pull, legs, twice, on the six days after your rest day. The time of day decides how long the session is and how busy the gym is likely to be. A new day starts at midnight. The AI Trainer follows built-in rules based on published guidance, not a live AI, and isn't medical advice. Your settings and workouts are stored only in this browser.
